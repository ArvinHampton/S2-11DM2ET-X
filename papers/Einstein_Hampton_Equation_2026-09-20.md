## Action

S = S_5 + S_partial + S_+ + S_- + S_D2 + S_m
S_5 = 1/(16 pi G_5) \int d^5x \sqrt-g (R - 2 \Lambda_5), \Lambda_5 = -6k^2
S_D2 = -T_2 \int d^3\xi \sqrt-\gamma, \xi = (t, x_parallel, y)

## Bulk tensor

T^{AB} = -2/\sqrt-g \delta S_D2/\delta g_AB
T^{tt} = T_2 e^{4kby} a^{-2} \delta^2(x_perp)
T^{yy} = -T_2 e^{2kby}/(a^2 b^2) \delta^2(x_perp)
T^{perp perp} = 0

Smear \delta^2 \to n_2. n_2 T_2 b_0 = \rho_DE(0).
